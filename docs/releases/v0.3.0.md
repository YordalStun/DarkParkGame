## Dark Park v0.3.0 - Clocking Out

Night one is now a full shift, from the phone call to walking out of the door, and your
coins carry over from night to night.

### New
- **Save system.** A title screen with Continue, New game and Load, and three save slots.
  The game saves between nights (when you press *Next night*); quitting part-way through a
  night restarts that night.
- **End of the night.** At 07:00: shut down the PC, sign the logbook, then **get up**. Walk
  around the booth (WASD + mouse), switch the light off by the door, and leave. The door
  creaks open and bangs shut as the night fades out.
- **End-of-shift screen.** Tonight's pay line by line, then the bills: tick what you pay
  (rent, food, heating, bus pass). No pass or fail; you carry on as long as you can cover them.
- **The back of the booth**, with a proper door, EXIT sign and light switch. It's lived-in on
  night one, and emptier and stranger after that.
- **Ten car designs** (saloon, hatch, supermini, estate, coupe, exec, 4x4, van, sports, MPV).
- **Cars leaving** through their own exit barrier; arrivals now come in a different order
  every night.
- **Two PC games**: Solitaire and NightSnake.
- A new 3am phone call, a stronger sunrise, objectives, coins, and speech sounds.

### Options
- The pause menu has **Sound**, **Graphics** and **Testing** tabs.
- Graphics: pixel size, frame cap, VSync, fullscreen, show FPS, mist, reflections, ambient
  occlusion, glow, shadows, 15-bit colour and film grain.
- Testing (F12): jump the clock, call the next car, go straight to clock-out or the end
  screen, change the weather, switch the booth's look, add coins.
- The build number is shown on the pause and title screens.

### Install
Download `DarkPark-windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere, and run `DarkPark.exe` from inside the folder.
