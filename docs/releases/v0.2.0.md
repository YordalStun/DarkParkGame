## Dark Park v0.2.0 - Night One

The first playable night. Friday 13th October 1989: the hospital car park has been flooded since Monday, and its night staff come through your gate at Lot C.

### New
- **The phone rings when you sit down.** Pick it up for Dale's briefing (click through each line).
- **Night staff arrive at the side window.** Click `<` (or the window) to turn to it, slide it open, and take their staff ID.
- **Check the card against tonight's list:** name on the list, card in date. Keep hold of it while you check something else, ask for it again, or refuse entry.
- **Read the plate and log it** in the new **GateLog** program on the PC.
- **Raise the barrier** with the green button and watch them drive in, park and walk off. Turned-away drivers reverse back down the lane.
- Seven arrivals, a couple who shouldn't get in, something at 3am that really shouldn't, and an end-of-night report.
- New, much better cars with dark tinted glass; a floodlight over the side window.

### Sound and controls
- Pause screen mixer: **Volume, Rain & Weather, Radio, Room & Lot, Cars** (saved between runs).
- **F10 or P** pauses from anywhere, including at the PC. Esc pauses from the desk.
- The camera holds still when leaned in to the CRT and while an ID card, plate or report is on screen.
- Car engines are much quieter.

### Also included
- WorldWire news site in the in-game browser (real 1995 headlines), real web pages and YouTube via the embedded browser, the 24h store, live CCTV, and all earlier fixes.

### Install
Download `DarkPark-windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere, and run `DarkPark.exe`.
