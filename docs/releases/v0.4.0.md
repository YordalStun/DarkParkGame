## Dark Park v0.4.0 - Night Two

Saturday 14th October 1989. The flood's still there, and tonight there's more to check.

### Night two
- **Plates must match.** Tonight's list shows each person's car. Click their car at the
  window to read the plate. If they turn up in a different car, they don't come in.
- **The ward rings mid-shift** with changes to the list. The printed list doesn't change,
  so write them down.
- **Notepad.** Press **N** (or click the yellow pad on the desk) and type. Your notes
  appear as a sticky beside every ID card you check.
- **Guide.** The objectives now say which button or key to use, and short TIP pop-ups
  explain anything new (each shows once per save).
- **CO-OP UNLOCKED** at the end of night two. From night three you'll be leaving the hut.
  (Until night three is built, the next night replays night two.)

### Also new since v0.3.1
- **Vehicle log clipboard**: an upgrade you can buy on the end-of-shift screen (4 coins).
  Once you have it, log plates right at the side window.
- **Calmer side-window view**, plus a **Camera motion** comfort option (Full / Reduced /
  Minimal) under Graphics.

### Install
Download `DarkPark-windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere, and run `DarkPark.exe` from inside the
folder. Your saves carry over.
