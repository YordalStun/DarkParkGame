# Dark Park v0.8.2

## Storage crate
- From night three, a wooden crate in the booth holds up to 10 items and keeps them between nights.
- Click the crate to put in or take out items you've taken from boots. Story items like Doris's newsletter can go in too.
- You can hide the crate under the desk. Click it again to bring it back out.

## The man by the payphone
- Nothing sells by itself at the end of the night any more.
- Some time in the night a man waits by the payphone. Go and talk to him, then tick the items in your crate you want to sell. He pays in coins, and each sale adds a bit of risk.

## Spot checks
- Sometimes the area supervisor drops in. You'll hear his van first, so you have about 20 seconds.
- If the crate is out, or you're carrying stolen goods, he takes them, reports you twice and your risk jumps.
