# Dark Park v0.8.8

- **A proper car alarm.** A new four-part 80s siren: rising whoops, a fast warble, a slow wail, then the horn. The lights flash in time with it and are much brighter: amber hazards, headlamps flashing side to side, and strobes.
- **The police walk round things.** The officer steers round parked cars, the booth, the payphone and the rest instead of walking through them.
- **The van's back doors open outwards** (before, they swung into the van).
- **Drivers fit in their cars.** Every type of car now seats its driver so their head, hair and hats stay under the roof and their knees clear the dashboard. The van's driver used to be sitting in the dash.
