## Dark Park v0.7.0 - Leave the hut

**From night three you can get up and go outside**, on your own or in co-op.

- **Get up:** press **G** (or the GET UP button in the corner). **WASD** to walk, mouse to look.
- **Go outside:** walk to the door at the back of the booth and press **E**. Press E on it again to come back in.
- **Sit back down:** **G** or right click, inside the booth.
- **Out in the car park:** you've got a torch, the rain falls round you, and you hear the lot as it is (not muffled through the glass). The fence, the tree line, the shop and the end of the access road keep you in the lot. Parked cars, the booth, the lamp island, the payphone and the bin are in the way, and a car driving in will nudge you aside.

### One desk chair
- **Only one guard can sit at a time.** In co-op P1 starts in the chair and everyone else starts on their feet. If the chair is taken you're told who's in it; sit down when it's free.
- **Standing, you can still use everything:** the phone, the side window (click it), the barrier buttons, the logbook and the PC (still one guard on the PC at a time). Leaning back out of the PC or the window puts you back on your feet where you were.

### Co-op
- You see the other guards walk about, with their torch on when they're outside, and the door swings when someone goes through it.
- Name tags (P1-P4) only show from a distance, so they're not in your face inside the booth.

### Clocking out
Works as before. If you're outside at 07:00, come back in first: shut the PC down, sign the logbook, light off, out of the door. If you're already standing there's no GET UP step.

Nights one and two are unchanged. Everything from v0.6.0 (co-op) is included.

### Install
Download `DarkPark-windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere, and run `DarkPark.exe` from inside the folder. Your saves carry over. Everyone playing together needs this same version.
