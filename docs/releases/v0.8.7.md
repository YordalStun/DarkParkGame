# Dark Park v0.8.7

## Boots, remade
- **Every kind of car opens properly.** Saloons lift a boot lid, and hatchbacks, estates, 4x4s and MPVs lift the whole tailgate. The van swings open two rear doors. They all open and close smoothly, with a clunk.
- **Look inside.** Click an open boot and you look down into it. Click things to take them, right-click to step back, and press E to close the boot.
- **Getting away with it.** The risk doesn't land straight away. Close the boot and walk off: with 1 thing taken there's a 70% chance nobody misses it, and with 2 it's 40%. With 3 or more, or from a van, it's always missed.
- **Leave a boot open** and the owner rings the booth, and your risk jumps.
- **Caught in the act.** If the police walk past while you've got your head in a boot, the night's lost.
- Dale brings up last night's boots in his call the next night, instead of ringing straight away.

## Vans
- Vans carry the best stuff, but every item adds more risk and you never get away clean.
- In co-op, a van's back doors need two guards standing there.

## Big things
- The guitar, golf clubs, fishing gear, suitcase and TV take 2 spaces in the crate. If you hide the crate under the desk, they still stick out, so the police and the supervisor can see them.

## Nite Stop
- It's a proper little shop now: a pick lock (2 coins) and the **BootScan** (3 coins), which shows what everything in a boot is worth.

## Baxter
- He holds the cigarette to his lips and the tip glows when he draws on it, then he lowers it and blows the smoke out.
- Press E on him to see his ID card.
- Fixed: he said there was nothing to sell when the crate was hidden.
