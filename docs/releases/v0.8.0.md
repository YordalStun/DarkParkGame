## Dark Park v0.8.0 - Doris's newsletter

### A new face on night three
- **Doris Fenn**, Ward 7's clerk: sixty-four, forty-one years at the General, retiring at Christmas. She's on tonight's list with her card in date, so let her in.
- At the side window she likes a chat (pick your replies), and before she goes she hands you a copy of the **staff newsletter**: she printed two by mistake. It lands on the desk; click it to read it.
- She has no idea what's on it. Read it carefully, **footer included**: the ward printer adds more than the date. The newsletter also mentions a **staff noticeboard** on the hospital's website that you won't find any other way.
- The leaked memo on the PC still works too, so if you miss Doris you're not stuck.

### Which night is it?
- **"NIGHT 3"** now sits beside your coins in the top corner all night.
- Each night opens with a short **"NIGHT THREE"** title and the date as it fades in. Works in co-op too.

### Fixed
- Night three showed night two's date (Saturday 14th). It's now **Sunday 15th October 1989** on the ID checks, the report and the title.

Everything from v0.7.0 (leaving the hut, the one desk chair, co-op) is included.

### Install
Download `DarkPark-windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere, and run `DarkPark.exe` from inside the folder. Your saves carry over. Everyone playing together needs this same version.
