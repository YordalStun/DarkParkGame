## Dark Park v0.8.1

### Quick fixes
- **The chair moves with you.** It rolls back when you get up, rolls in when you sit, swivels as you look around, and follows you when you lean to the side window or in to the screen. In co-op, everyone sees it move.
- **Smaller plate view**, which only opens when you click the number plate itself.

### Staying alive (single player)
- **Heat, food and rent** each have a daily cost: rent 2, food 1, heat 1. The 8-coin bank covers the first two days.
- **Each bill is a 3-pip meter**, shown on the HUD and on the end-of-shift screen. Paying refills it; skipping a night costs one pip. At zero it's game over: evicted, starving or too cold.
- **Reports** to Dale are saved. At 5 Dale's suspicious, at 7 it's his last warning, at 10 you're fired. One is forgiven every two nights.
- **Risk** is saved and drops 20% a day. If it's at 100 at the end of a night you're caught, and the night restarts with your reports kept.

### Car boots (night three on)
- **Buy a boot wedge** (2 coins) on the end-of-shift screen.
- **Every car has a real boot** with a hinged lid and a boot light, holding up to 5 things (20 kinds). Opening one gets you reported.
- **Taking an item** adds risk, and you can carry 3 slots. Drop things in the **storage crate** in the hut.
- **The man by the payphone** buys the lot at the end of the night.

### Night three
- **Its own people and conversations:** Kevin's dog Biscuit (the hospital password), Tom's guitar, Dr Marsh's husband's camera bag, and Mick Doyle turning up in the wrong car.

### Install
Download `DarkPark-windows.zip`, unzip it, and run `DarkPark.exe` from inside the folder. Your saves carry over.
