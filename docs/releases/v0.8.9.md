# Dark Park v0.8.9

## Co-op: boots work for everyone
- When anyone opens or closes a boot, every guard sees the lid move.
- Everyone sees the same things inside. When someone takes one, it goes into their hands and disappears from the boot on every screen.
- The risk counts for the whole team. A boot only counts as walked away from once every guard has left that car.
- If the police catch anyone with their head in a boot, the night's lost for everyone.
- Visitors' cars can be opened by guests too, not just the host.

Both players need v0.8.9 to play together.
