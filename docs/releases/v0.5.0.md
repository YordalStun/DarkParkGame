## Dark Park v0.5.0 - Night Three

- **Selectable dialog.** Conversations now give you 2-4 numbered replies (click or press 1-4) with a 20 second timer. If it runs out, the safe reply is chosen for you and the screen says so.
- **The hospital side hustle (night three).** A leaked IT memo gives you the hospital gateway's address. Dial it from the PC with the new NightLink app, guess a login from what the visitors tell you, and download files. Every move raises a risk-of-being-caught meter.
- **Night two touches.** Rain-damaged ID cards and lists, a wet sill and puddle if you leave the side window open, coffee that goes cold, a radio that crackles in heavy rain, a louder clock at 02:00, and the last operator's notes in the logbook.
- **The Ashford Gazette** has a new story about a hacker selling stolen hospital records (nights two and three).
- **Fixes.** No more soft lock after the ward's phone call on night two. The radio aerial no longer pokes through the window sill, the mug is properly hollow, and drivers of low cars no longer clip through the roof.
- **Testing.** The Testing menu (F12) can jump straight to night one, two or three.
- **Four player guard models** are in, ready for co-op.

Everything from v0.4.1 (the hospital, umbrellas, brighter CCTV) is included.

### Install
Download `DarkPark-windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere, and run `DarkPark.exe` from inside the folder. Your saves carry over.
