# Dark Park v0.8.5

## Co-op
- **Everyone sees the PC screen.** Whoever is using a computer shares its screen, so the other guards' monitors show what they're doing.
- **The second hut's PC works.** Once the hut's open you can walk in, sit at the old PC and use it. It's a whole separate computer, with its own windows and files, so two guards can be on two PCs at once. Its screen is shared with everyone too.
- **The testing menu works in co-op.** When a guest presses a button, the host does it, so everyone gets the same result.
- **New test buttons (NIGHT THREE ON)**: unlock the second hut and its PC, set off the car alarm, send in the police, bring Baxter to the payphone, fill the crate, get a free pick lock. There's also a jump to night 4.

Both players need v0.8.5 to play together. Older versions can't join.
