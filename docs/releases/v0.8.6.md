# Dark Park v0.8.6

## Fixes
- **Things in the car park now work when you're outside.** Car boots, the things inside them, Baxter, the police officer, the alarm car and its note, the Nite Stop and the old hut's door and PC couldn't be clicked from outside before. Now they can.
- **Baxter has moved.** He waits at the back of the lot, by the fence near the old hut, instead of right next to the booth. He's smoking, so look for the glowing cigarette in the dark. He also looks like a shabby records clerk now, not a porter. The note and his line at the window tell you where to find him.
- **Co-op: your friend's legs move properly when they walk.**

## Co-op money
- No rent, food or heat in co-op. The end-of-night screen shows the **team quota** instead: what you needed and what you brought in. The quota is just a target, so you keep every coin and spend it on things to buy.
- Things the team buys (boot wedge, pick lock, vehicle log) are everyone's.

## Testing menu
- **Boot wedge (free)**: open car boots straight away.
- The old hut's door says "Pick the lock" when you've got a pick lock.

Both players need v0.8.6 to play together.
