# Dark Park v0.8.3

## Night three
- **Required tasks**: from night three, the objectives in the top right are tasks you have to do. They stay on the list, night after night, until they're done:
  - inspect IDs and plates
  - take the call that adds someone to the list
  - get the printed sheet
  - find out why the car alarm went off
  - buy a pick lock
- **About six cars**, picked from a rotating pool each night. Kevin always comes (and his spaniel, Biscuit). Doris comes after Ward 7 rings to add her to the list.
- **No pay on night three**: payroll's frozen. Mistakes still cost you.
- **The alarm car**: a random parked car goes off, with the hazards flashing, the headlamps wig-wagging and the horn going. Go out and check it. There's nothing wrong with it, but there's a note on the ground...
- **Baxter** (Medical Records, and clearly unwell) turns up later in the night once you've found the note. Bring it up at the window and he'll want to meet you at the payphone, where you can offer to sell him things. From then on he's your buyer.
- **The police** walk the lot with a torch after the second or third car. You can walk up and talk to them. From night four, if they spot a car with its boot open, they come and look in your hut. Keep the crate hidden and don't leave the PC downloading, or the night's lost.
- **Nite Stop**: walk to the shop's door to buy a pick lock (2 coins). It can snap.
- On night three, opening a boot doesn't get you reported. Dale rings and asks you to keep an eye out for stolen goods. The area supervisor's spot checks start on night four.

## Co-op
- If you're not in a conversation, you can follow it (and the options being picked) in a small box under the objectives.
- The old second hut at the back of the lot. It's padlocked, so buy a pick lock and break in the next night. One pick lock is enough for the whole team.
- A nightly quota: 4 coins from what the night brings in (more every three nights). If you miss it, you play the night again.

## Fixes
- Loading a save now keeps the storage crate, whether it's hidden, and story progress. Before, these were lost when you loaded a game.
