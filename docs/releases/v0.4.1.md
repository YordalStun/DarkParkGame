## Dark Park v0.4.1 - Umbrellas

- **The hospital.** Ashford General's A&E now stands at the end of the aisle, lit up through the rain.
- **Staff walk in under umbrellas.** After parking, each driver gets out, their umbrella springs open, and they walk off to the hospital.
- **Brighter CCTV** on the PC's CamLink feeds, so you can actually see what the cameras see.

Everything from v0.4.0 (night two, the notepad, the vehicle log clipboard) is included.

### Install
Download `DarkPark-windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere, and run `DarkPark.exe` from inside the folder. Your saves carry over.
