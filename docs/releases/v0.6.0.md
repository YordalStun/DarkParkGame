## Dark Park v0.6.0 - Co-op

**Up to four guards can now share the night shift.**

- **MULTIPLAYER on the title screen.** Co-op unlocks when you finish night two, and night three is the first co-op night.
- **Haven't got that far yet?** You can still host: it starts a **new game that skips straight to night three**, in a save slot you pick. Your other saves are left alone. Joining a friend needs nothing unlocked.
- **Hosting:** pick a save and you get a lobby showing your **IP address and port** (24680). When everyone's in, press **START**.
- **Joining:** type the host's IP address and port. The game remembers the last address you used.
- **Same house / same network:** that's all you need. **Over the internet:** the host forwards **UDP port 24680** on their router to their PC, and friends use the host's public IP. You can also all join a virtual LAN like Hamachi or ZeroTier. The first time you host, Windows may ask whether to let Dark Park through the firewall: allow it.
- **Friends join from the lobby only.** Once the night has started, nobody else can join.

### Playing together
- The **host runs the night**, and **only the host's save changes**. Friends play as guests and keep nothing.
- **P1 sits at the desk**; P2, P3 and P4 stand around it, each with their own colour and a number on their back.
- **Anyone** can answer the phone, open the side window, take an ID card, and raise or lower the barrier.
- **Whoever opened the window (or answered the phone) picks the replies.** Everyone else sees the options and who is choosing.
- **One guard at a time on the PC.** Whoever sits down at it first has it until they sit back.
- **Shared results:** one team pot of coins and one report. The hospital hack's risk meter rises faster with more guards.
- **At 07:00 the host clocks out.** Everyone sees the same report, and only the host presses NEXT NIGHT, which takes everyone into the next night together.
- If the **host leaves**, everyone goes back to the title screen. If a **friend leaves**, their guard disappears and whatever they were using is freed.

Everything from v0.5.1 (the MediCare Remote install, the night-jump crash fix, the mouse on the end screen) is included.

### Known limits
- Guards who aren't on the PC can't see its screen yet; they're told who is using it.
- Co-op has been tested with two copies of the game on one PC. Please report how it goes between two PCs.

### Install
Download `DarkPark-windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere, and run `DarkPark.exe` from inside the folder. Your saves carry over. Everyone playing together needs this same version.
