## Dark Park v0.3.1

Small fixes to the end of the shift.

- **You have a chair.** An 80s office swivel chair at the desk. When you get up it rolls back and swivels out of the way, and you walk round it.
- **Standing height** is lower (1.5 m), so the booth no longer feels too small for you.
- **Leaving needs a key press.** Look at the door and press **E**. Clicking the door only shows a reminder, so you can't walk out by accident. E also works the light switch.

### Install
Download `DarkPark-windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere, and run `DarkPark.exe` from inside the folder. Your saves carry over.
