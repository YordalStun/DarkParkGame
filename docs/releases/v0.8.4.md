# Dark Park v0.8.4

## Co-op
- **Baxter is there for everyone.** Every guard sees him waiting at the payphone, and anyone can walk up, talk to him and sell. The guard who walks up gets the sale menu on their own screen.
- **One crate for the team.** The storage crate in the hut is shared, and it's kept in the host's save. Anyone can put stolen things in, take them out, hide it under the desk, or sell from it. Whatever a guard is holding at the end of the night goes in the crate.
- The sale menu is now titled "BAXTER".
