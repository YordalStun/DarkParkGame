# Night three: lore and what the player does

Status: DRAFT for discussion. Night three is the start of the real game. Nights one and two
are a tutorial. From night three the player is free to choose: stay legal, or get into the
hacking and stealing.

## What the player is doing

- Keeping Dave alive: heat, food and rent (see core-loop.md). The job pays the bills, but
  it gets harder.
- Staying out of trouble: Dale's reports and the police risk meter (see core-loop.md).
- Choosing how far to go. Doing the legal job well, or dipping into the PC and the
  stolen-goods side for the big money.

## What is already in the game for night three

- **Doris Fenn** (Ward 7 clerk, 64, retiring at Christmas). Hands Dave the staff newsletter.
  The newsletter's footer shows the hospital gateway address, and the newsletter points
  to the staff noticeboard with the MediCare Remote download.
- **The leaked I.T. memo** on the PC gives the same gateway address, so either route works.
- **The hospital login** (NightLink). Needs a password, which is a clue from an NPC.
- **Kessler** still comes back on night two. He's the car in row C that hasn't left.
- **The weird guy** (the stolen-goods buyer / dark web contact). Not yet written.

## Night three beats (draft)

1. Dale's call: the job is the same, but Dale mentions the new arrivals and the
   reporting rules. Dale does not suspect Dave yet.
2. Arrivals come in as normal. Each one has a short dialog, with a couple of lines for
   lore and a couple of hints that the hacking or stealing is possible.
3. Doris arrives. Her newsletter gives the gateway address and the noticeboard.
4. Night three people with their own conversations (each one gives a clue, a hint, or
   a choice). One has a dog, whose name is the password clue.
5. The first report, if the player opens a boot. Dale's reaction stays light at first.
6. The weird guy shows up in the lot once the player has something to sell or a reason to
   want something (open question).
7. End of the night: the bills are paid, and the report says what the player got done.

## Night three: decisions (from the owner)

- Night three is about six cars. Night three is the first night things get hard.
- Night three earns no money. The player must make something give at the end of the
  night. In multiplayer, the team loses on the first quota.
- **Police walk-through:** early on, police walk through the lot with a torch after about
  the second or third car. They don't stop anyone and they only walk through.
  - The player can leave the chair and talk to them. Early on they find nothing illegal.
  - Later, if the police walk near a car with an open boot, they come over to the booth
    and look inside. A booth that's covered is fine. If the storage box is showing, or the
    PC is downloading, the player loses that night.
- **Objectives from night three:** the objectives in the top right are no longer tutorial
  text. They're tasks the player must finish to progress, and they stay until done.
- **Alarm car:** a car alarm goes off on night three. The lights on the car are going
  mad. Checking it shows nothing, but the player finds a note with "dealer" and a name.
  - The name is the weird guy. He works at the hospital as a non-clinical admin, and he
    is clearly unwell.
- **The weird guy:** once the player has the note, they can bring it up with him. At first
  he is scared. On the next talk, the player can offer to sell him something, which opens
  up the stolen-goods menu. Night three is too early for anything to sell.
- **Reporting on night three:** a report is very unlikely. If the player is stealing, Dale
  only asks them to keep an eye out for stolen goods.
- **No ending:** the game keeps going after night three.

## What the player must do on night three

1. Inspect IDs and plates at the window.
2. Take calls that add people to the list.
3. Get the printed document with the web address and IP on it.
4. Find the note about the dealer near the car making the loud noise.
5. Buy the pick lock from the store. In multiplayer, the second hut is there from the
   start and the players are asked to look around it. They can't get in until they buy
   the pick lock, and then they can break in the next day.

## Answers to the night three questions

- **Dog owner: Kevin Platt.** Matches the existing spaniel clue ("Biscuit"). The password
  clue for the hospital login is Biscuit, learned from Kevin at the window.
- **Weird guy: Baxter (Tom Baxter).** Works in the hospital's non-clinical admin. His name
  may be revealed more fully later.
- **Dealer note:** the note found after the alarm car names Baxter as the "dealer".
- **Name rotation:** night three uses a rotating pool of arrivals, not a fixed list. After
  the player finds the dealer note, Baxter is the fifth car (the maybe-timing is still to
  check). A dialog option to bring up the note is available as long as the player has it.
- **Police:** they are in the world. They walk the lot with a torch and can be talked to
  by walking up to them.
- **Pick lock: 2 coins.** It can break. In multiplayer, once one player buys it, both
  have it.
